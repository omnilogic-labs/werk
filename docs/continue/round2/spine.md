You are one of four independent attempts. Three things the page has to land, and
they are the spine of the pitch:

1. **Starting out, on your own.** What it is actually like the first time: you
   dispatch from your own terminal, the work runs somewhere else, you shut the
   laptop, and you come back to the session later and it is still there.
2. **Sharing a terminal.** Handing a colleague a live link to one of your
   sessions, and the two of you in it.
3. **The company-wide view.** Every agent session in the organisation on one
   view — each person, the sessions they are running, the subagents those
   sessions have spawned — and opening any one of them onto its live terminal.

The first exists today. The other two are planned and not built; show them
properly and be accurate about which is which.

These three are also what your product images should show.
